Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

RV Foods is a homemade food business based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. We sell Diwali faral, sweets, snacks, masale and ghee through this website, and customers place their orders over WhatsApp. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and who it is shared with.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your phone number if you add one. If you register with a password, we store it only in a hashed form, not as readable text.
  • Google sign-in details: if you choose to sign in with Google, see the section below.
  • Delivery details: the address you search for, the flat and building details and PIN code you add, your name if you give it, and the map location of your delivery point.
  • Order details: the products you order, prices, delivery charge, order status and payment status.
  • Reviews: the rating and comment you submit for a product.

We do not collect or store card or bank details on this website. Payments are made by UPI using your own payment app, or as agreed with us on WhatsApp.

Google sign-in and Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We use this information only to:

  • create your RV Foods account or link it to an existing account with the same email;
  • sign you in and keep you signed in;
  • show your name on your account and use it on your orders.

We store these details with your account in our database. We do not sell Google user data, do not share it with third parties except the service providers listed below that run this website, and do not use it for advertising. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

RV Foods' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove our access at any time from your Google Account connections page.

How we use your information

  • To work out your approximate delivery charge from the road distance to your address.
  • To record, prepare and deliver your order, and to contact you about it.
  • To send account emails, such as verification codes and password resets.
  • To let you sign in, see your past orders and leave reviews.
  • To run and protect the website, for example by limiting repeated requests.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use only what the site needs to work:

  • A secure cookie that keeps you signed in.
  • Your browser's local storage, to remember your cart and your signed-in session.
  • Your browser's session storage, to remember an order you have just started on WhatsApp.

Google may set its own cookies when you use the Google sign-in button.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is open. We keep order records for as long as we need them to run the business and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing us at the address below. We may need to keep some order records where the law requires it.

Keeping information safe

We use secure (HTTPS) connections on the live site and limit administrator access to order and account information. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

This website is meant for adults placing food orders, and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
